# Name and logo

OpenWren's code is open source under the Apache License 2.0. The licence covers the code. It does not grant rights to the project's name or logo (see section 6 of the licence).

"OpenWren" and the wren logo identify this project and the builds it publishes.

**You may, without asking:**

- Say that your work uses, is based on, or is compatible with OpenWren.
- Use the name and logo to refer to this project, for example in articles, reviews and talks.
- Package the unmodified app for a distribution under its name.

**Please do not:**

- Distribute a modified version under the name OpenWren or with its logo. A fork needs its own name and icon.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests the project endorses or runs your product or service.
- Register the name, the logo or something confusingly similar as a trademark, domain or account name.

For anything else, ask by opening an issue.

The company logos shown for well-known senders come from the Simple Icons set and are trademarks of their owners. They are shown only to identify who a message is from.
